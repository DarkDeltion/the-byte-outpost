# Witte achtergronden voor productfoto's

Methode: de ingebouwde image_gen-tool, bewerking van de bestaande productafbeeldingen. De oorspronkelijke bronfoto's zijn bewaard. De website gebruikt de nieuwe WebP-varianten om ook bestaande browsercaches van de oude foto te vermijden.

## ipad-pro-m4

- Origineel: `public/images/products/ipad-pro-m4.webp`
- Nieuwe variant: `public/images/products/ipad-pro-m4-white.webp`

Gebruikte prompt:

Use case: precise-object-edit. Asset type: existing ecommerce product photo background replacement. Edit target: the referenced image. Replace ONLY the entire empty background with uniform pure white #FFFFFF, removing the visible grey or cream rectangular matte. Preserve the Apple iPad Pro M4 front-and-back product render, including the black display with its exact colourful luminous abstract wallpaper, rear camera, casing, angle, scale, and composition. Keep all product pixels, colours, text, edges, details and spacing unchanged as closely as possible. Keep the whole device visible, with the same square canvas composition and framing. Do not redesign or regenerate the product. No extra objects, borders, gradients, shadows, text or watermarks. This must blend invisibly into a pure-white product-card background.
